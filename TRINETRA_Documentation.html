TRINETRA DOCUMENTATION

Adaptive AI Digital Trust Agent — a user-controlled trust layer that helps people inspect suspicious media, information and identity-related threats before they believe, share or act on them.

Explainable analysisUser-controlledCross-channel contextAdaptive trust memory
The central idea

Instead of asking users to open a different security app for every suspicious item, TRINETRA brings an investigation assistant closer to the content they are viewing — then combines multiple evidence signals into one understandable report.

TMTrustMemoryRemember and match⌄
Stores investigation fingerprints and historical cases. Exact cryptographic matches are kept separate from approximate visual or semantic similarity. A similar item must not automatically inherit another item's verdict.
CICommunity IntelligenceEvidence from people⌄
Lets users submit evidence, comments and votes, while reviewer reputation and moderation add context. Popularity or majority votes are not treated as ground truth.
TGTrustGraphConnect related cases⌄
Maps explainable relationships among media, claims, people, websites, payment destinations and campaigns. It helps reveal patterns that a single isolated scan may miss.
ILIntentLockProtect a chosen action⌄
For supported in-app flows, approval is tied to the exact action details. If the amount, beneficiary or destination changes, the old approval becomes invalid and the user must review again. This is not universal banking integration.
TLTrustLedgerTamper-evident audit⌄
Maintains an append-only, hash-linked record of investigation events so tampering can be detected. Sensitive media is not placed on a public blockchain.
ALControlled AdaptationImprove responsibly⌄
The system can improve through verified intelligence and curated, consented datasets with controlled model releases. Raw community votes do not automatically retrain production models.

The three investigation engines are the main user choices. The shared intelligence layer supports them; it is not a fourth i-Ball option.

3. What makes TRINETRA more than a scanner?

A suspicious item can be compared with previous investigations, with exact matches clearly distinguished from approximate matches.
02Evidence, not popularityCommunity signals with moderation⌄
Community reports can add useful context, but evidence quality, verification and reviewer reputation matter more than vote count alone.

Different clues can be connected into a campaign view — for example, a repeated claim, a lookalike domain and a payment destination.

4. TRINETRA innovation — how our approach is different

Compare the common approach in existing tool categories with the connected approach TRINETRA is designed to provide.

Click each comparison box to expand ↓
This is a category-level comparison of capabilities, not a claim that every existing product has the same limitations. Specialist tools can be excellent at their individual tasks. TRINETRA's proposed innovation is bringing several investigations and their context into one connected workflow.
01Standalone deepfake / media detectorsUsually focus on one media item⌄
Common approach: Analyse an image, audio clip or video and return a detector result or score. The user may still need separate tools to check the related claim, source or website.

TRINETRA's intended difference: MediaGuard is one of three investigation paths. Its findings can be considered alongside provenance, related claims and historical cases through the shared intelligence layer.
02Fact-checking websites and searchOften require manual claim searching⌄
Common approach: A user copies a claim, searches for it and reads available fact-checks or source pages. This can be useful, but the user has to assemble context across pages and services.

TRINETRA's intended difference: OriginTrace is designed to extract claims and relevant details, examine source and context signals, and connect related investigations instead of presenting an isolated search result.
03URL and phishing scannersPrimarily inspect links and domains⌄
Common approach: Check a URL or domain against reputation, blocklists or technical indicators. They are useful for link safety, but may not investigate the message, media or claim that delivered the link.

TRINETRA's intended difference: IdentityGuard checks link, QR, message and impersonation signals, while the wider design can relate a suspicious destination to other evidence and cases.
04Community reporting platformsCan surface user-reported warnings⌄
Common approach: Users report, comment or vote on suspicious content. Community signals can be helpful, but popularity alone does not prove that a claim is true or false.

TRINETRA's intended difference: Community Intelligence is designed to add evidence, reviewer context and moderation. Votes are treated as signals, not ground truth, and do not automatically train production models.
05Separate security apps and workflowsUsers switch between different tools⌄
Common approach: A person may use one app for media, another for fact-checking and another for link reputation, then manually compare the results.

TRINETRA's intended difference: The i-Ball provides one user-controlled entry point with exactly three investigation choices and a shared report/intelligence model. It aims to reduce tool switching and make evidence easier to understand.
06One-time warnings and verdictsMay not explain what happens next⌄
Common approach: A tool may warn about a single item, but the user still has to decide what to do and remember previous incidents independently.

TRINETRA's intended difference: TrustMemory and TrustGraph are designed to connect related cases; IntentLock adds a re-check for supported in-app actions when important details change; TrustLedger supports tamper-evident audit history.

These ideas combine into one user journey rather than six unrelated features.

→
Step 2

Three specialist paths

→
Step 3

Shared intelligence

Memory, community evidence and relationship mapping add context.

Innovation A

Evidence fusion

Explain the signals and uncertainty, not just a simple real/fake label.

Innovation B

Cross-case context

Connect related media, claims, domains and campaigns where evidence supports a link.

Innovation C

Action-aware trust

Help users review supported sensitive actions and preserve an auditable record.

The differentiator is the combination: a user-controlled interface, three focused investigation engines, shared cross-investigation context and safer action review. It is a proposed system-level innovation, not a claim of proven higher detection accuracy than every specialist product.
